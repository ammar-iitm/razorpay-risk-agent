Day 2 — local test checkout (not for production, not published anywhere)

Serve this over localhost, don't open it as a file:// URL — Razorpay's checkout widget talks to a popup via postMessage, and a file:// page has a null origin, which breaks that silently with a generic "test failed" error that has nothing to do with your keys. Run python3 -m http.server 8000 in this folder and open http://localhost:8000/day2/checkout.html instead.

This never touches a server of mine — it loads Razorpay's own checkout.js directly in your browser.
Result will appear here after you click Success or Failure on the mock bank page.
